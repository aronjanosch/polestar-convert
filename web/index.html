polestar-convert

Your RestedXP, Journey and Zygor guides as a Polestar guide pack, converted in your browser.

Nothing leaves your computer. This page reads the guide addons in the folder you pick, right here in your browser, and hands you the pack as a ZIP. Your browser may ask whether to “upload” the files: that is its word for letting this page read them; nothing is sent anywhere. The page only downloads Polestar's game data from this site. It ships no guide content: the guides are their authors' work, so keep the pack to yourself.

1 Pick your AddOns folder

Your client's Interface/AddOns folder, for example World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns. The World of Warcraft folder works too.

    2 What to convert

    Every guide addon found in the folder. RestedXP holds four routes side by side; most players run one, so pick the ones you want. Journey and Zygor each have one route and come whole.

    3 Your pack

    The id names the pack's folder and its guides in Polestar.
